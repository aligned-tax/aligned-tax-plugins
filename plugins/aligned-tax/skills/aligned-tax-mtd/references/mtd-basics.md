# MTD for Income Tax basics

Facts as of 29 October 2026, including the Budget 2026 announcements of 28 October 2026, whose legislation is still pending. HMRC changes the rules from time to time; where a person's situation matters, suggest they check GOV.UK or with an accountant.

## Who is in

Sole traders and landlords whose qualifying income (self-employment plus property, before expenses) is over:

| From | Qualifying income | Based on |
|---|---|---|
| 6 April 2026 | over £50,000 | 2024-25 tax return |
| 6 April 2027 | over £30,000 | 2025-26 tax return |
| 6 April 2028 | over £20,000 | 2026-27 tax return |

## Quarterly updates

Four updates a year for each business. Each one is cumulative: it covers 6 April to the end of that quarter and replaces the previous update.

| Quarter | Standard period | Calendar period (optional) | Deadline |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | 6 April to 5 July | 6 April to 30 June (1 April after the first year) | 7 August |
| Q2 | 6 July to 5 October | 1 July to 30 September | 7 November |
| Q3 | 6 October to 5 January | 1 October to 31 December | 7 February |
| Q4 | 6 January to 5 April | 1 January to 31 March | 7 May |

Quarterly updates are summaries of income and expenses, not tax returns. A mistake is corrected in the next update. Updates can be sent more often than quarterly.

## Final Declaration

Replaces the Self Assessment tax return. Due by 31 January after the tax year ends, so 31 January 2028 for 2026-27. All quarterly updates must be in first. It is never done through the connector, and year end in aligned.tax is not yet open to customers; say so if asked, rather than promising it in the app.

## Late filing penalties

For people required to use MTD, each missed deadline earns one penalty point. At 4 points there is a £200 penalty, and £200 more for each further miss while at the threshold.

Volunteers (people using MTD before they are required to) are different: HMRC gives no penalty points for late quarterly updates while they are volunteering, but late tax returns still earn points and their threshold is 2 points, not 4 (GOV.UK, "Send quarterly updates", checked 29 October 2026).

No points are given for late quarterly updates in tax year 2026-27. Late Final Declarations still earn points. For people who joined in April 2026, late quarterly updates earn points from 2027-28. Budget 2026 (28 October 2026) announced, with legislation pending, that people required to join in April 2027 will get no points for late quarterly updates in 2027-28; they must still send them before their tax return. Do not tell someone a late 2026-27 quarterly update, or an April 2027 joiner's late 2027-28 update, will cost them points.

Budget 2026 also announced, with legislation pending, that people eligible for qualifying care relief, non-UK resident foreign entertainers and sportspeople, and people expected to be non-UK resident will be exempt from MTD for Income Tax until further notice.

## Digital records

Figures sent to HMRC must come from digital records kept in software or a spreadsheet, linked digitally to the submission. That is why you never type or adjust figures yourself.

## Words to use

- "HMRC-recognised software", never "HMRC-approved".
- "Quarterly update", not "quarterly return".
- "Final Declaration", not "tax return", for MTD years.
